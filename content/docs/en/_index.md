## Start here

New to this CRM? Work through [signing in](getting-started/sign-in), then
[finding your way around](getting-started/find-your-way). Both take a few minutes and neither assumes you
have used this product before.

If you are here to fix something, [search the handbook](/docs/search) for the word on your screen. If you
are here to change how the CRM behaves for everyone, open the
[administrator handbook](admin/settings-overview).
