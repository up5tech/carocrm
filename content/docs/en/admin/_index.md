## What is in this section

The six pages that cover the screens an administrator actually opens:
[the administration area](settings-overview), [branding, language and time](branding-and-localization),
[the main menu](main-menu), [users, teams and roles](users-teams-roles), [API keys](api-keys) and
[entities, fields and layouts](entities-and-fields).

Two things are true across all of them. Every change is enforced by the server, not by what the screen
shows you. And every setting belongs to a document that is saved as a whole, with one **Publish** button —
there is no autosave.

> [!NOTE]
> This handbook describes the product. It does not describe your instance's data, and it is never a
> substitute for a permission check: a page you can read here does not mean a screen you can open.
