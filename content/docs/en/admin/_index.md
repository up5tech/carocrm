## What is in this section

Start with [the administration area](settings-overview) — the tab model, the save bar and who may open
what — and the [first-run checklist](first-run) if this instance is new. The six pages that cover the
screens an administrator opens most: [branding, language and time](branding-and-localization),
[the main menu](main-menu), [users, teams and roles](users-teams-roles), [API keys](api-keys),
[entities, fields and layouts](entities-and-fields) and [authentication and security](authentication-and-security).

The rest cover one area each: [email and notifications](email-and-notifications),
[files and storage](files-and-storage), [workflows and processes](workflows-and-processes),
[extensions](extensions), [integrations](integrations), [module settings](module-settings),
[portals](portals), the [audit log, jobs and operations](audit-jobs-and-operations), and a configuration
guide for each module: [Sales](sales), [Service Desk](service-desk), [Customer Success](customer-success),
[Lead Score](lead-score), [Marketing](marketing), [Voice and messaging](voice), [CRM Chat](crm-chat) and
[AI agents](ai-agents).

Two things are true across all of them. Every change is enforced by the server, not by what the screen
shows you. And every setting belongs to a document that is saved as a whole, with one **Save** button —
there is no autosave.

> [!NOTE]
> This handbook describes the product. It does not describe your instance's data, and it is never a
> substitute for a permission check: a page you can read here does not mean a screen you can open.
