## What is in this section

The seven pages that cover the work most people do here:
[working with lists](lists-and-views), [reading and editing a record](record-page),
[activities, tasks and the calendar](activities-and-tasks),
[stream and notifications](stream-and-notifications), [search and the command palette](search),
[dashboards and reports](dashboards-and-reports) and [importing and exporting CSV](import-export).

The rest cover one module each, for the days that work is what you have to do:
[quotes and orders](sales-quotes), [cases, queues and SLAs](service-desk-cases), [projects](projects),
[customer success](customer-success), [campaigns and audiences](marketing),
[CRM Chat and the voice inbox](chat-and-voice), [signature requests](esignature), and the
[AI agents](ai-agents) you can run.

Each page describes the screens as they behave today, including the limits that catch people out.
