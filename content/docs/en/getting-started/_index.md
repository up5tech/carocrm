## What is in this section

Five short pages that get you working: [signing in and out](sign-in),
[finding your way around](find-your-way), [the home workspace](home), [your profile, language and time
zone](your-profile) and [keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts).

Everything here describes the screens as they are today. If a control the handbook names is missing from
your instance, your administrator has not granted you the permission for it — see
[users, teams and roles](admin/users-teams-roles).
