## The sidebar

The column on the left is where the CRM lives. Entries are grouped — your own pages at the top, then
records such as Accounts and Contacts, then activities, then the modules your instance has switched on.
Your administrator can rename, reorder, recolor or hide entries, so your sidebar may not look like a
screenshot you were sent. What matters is what you are allowed to see: an entry appears only when you have
permission for the screen behind it.

The whole sidebar can be collapsed to icons with the button at its top. With the keyboard, focus that
button and press **Enter** or **Space**; press **Escape** to close the drawer on a phone.

## The header search

The box at the top searches **records**. Type a name or an email address and press **Enter** to open the
[results page](/search). Nothing happens until you press Enter, and an empty box never searches.

## The avatar menu

Your picture in the top right opens three items:

- **My profile** — your name, contact details, [language and time zone](getting-started/your-profile).
- **Language** — switch between the languages your instance offers. The change applies immediately.
- **Sign out** — end this session.

## The command palette

Press **⌘K** on a Mac or **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux to open the palette; press it again to close it.
It has three modes, chosen with the tabs at the top:

| Mode | What it finds |
| --- | --- |
| Commands | The screens you use most: home, search, dashboards, reports, stream, calendar, my tasks, administration. |
| Records | The list page of an entity — it takes you to Contacts, not to a specific contact. |
| Recent | Pages you visited recently. |

Type to narrow the list, then press Enter to go. The palette navigates; it does not create records and does
not run any other command.
