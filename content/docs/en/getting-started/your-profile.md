Open your profile with the **My profile** item in the [avatar menu](getting-started/find-your-way), or go
straight to [/profile](/profile). It holds four cards, and each one saves on its own **Save** button —
saving your time zone does not touch your phone number.

## Your details

**First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Job title** are what your colleagues see on
records and in the stream. The sign-in name is shown below them and cannot be edited here; only an
administrator changes it.

## Your picture

Select **Upload** on the Avatar card and choose an image. **Remove** puts the initials back. Only an image
file you uploaded through this dialog is accepted.

## Language and time zone

Two selects decide how the interface reads and how dates appear to you:

- **Language** — the list is the languages your instance has switched on, not every language that exists.
- **Time zone** — your own zone is offered first, together with the zones commonly used. Leave it empty to
  follow the instance default.

> [!TIP]
> Switching language from the avatar menu applies immediately and is saved to the same setting as this
> card. Use whichever is closer.

Time zone changes alter how timestamps are displayed to you. They do not move a deadline that was already
stored: an activity at 09:00 UTC is the same instant in every zone.

## Change password

The fourth card takes your current password and the new one twice, with a minimum of eight characters.
Changing it signs every device out, so plan for a moment of re-signing in. See
[signing in and out](getting-started/sign-in).

## What is not here

There is no dark mode or theme control, no notification preferences and no email signature on this screen.
Teams and roles belong to an administrator, not to you — see
[users, teams and roles](admin/users-teams-roles).
